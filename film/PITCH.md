# OFF-MENU — One-Page Pitch

> *"In a world that plans your every move, the bravest thing you can do is make a mistake."*

---

**Title:** OFF-MENU
**Genre:** Sci-fi romantic comedy
**Tone:** *Her* × *About Time* × Korean rom-com
**Format:** Feature film (~100 min) / limited series pilot
**Setting:** Near-future Seoul

---

### The Hook
An AI called **CONTINUITY** pre-plans everyone's day — your coffee, your commute,
your small talk — so life has zero friction and zero risk. It also quietly killed
romance: why flirt when the algorithm already booked your wedding?

Then, at **08:47**, a coffee is ordered to a stranger's table that the system never
predicted. A *Wrong Order*. An act of pure free will. And someone did it **on
purpose** — to meet him.

### The Heart
**Jin-woo**, the analyst whose job is to make reality match the forecast, falls for
a glitch before he ever meets the woman behind it. **Soo-ah**, a "ghost" invisible
to the AI, is terrified of being predictable around anyone. The only way they can
find each other is to make increasingly terrible, un-forecast-able choices — order
the worst thing on the menu, ride to the wrong stop, crash the wrong wedding.

### The Stakes
CONTINUITY *quarantines* couples it can't predict. To beat it, a meddling friend
group of ghosts places one synchronized Wrong Order across the whole city at 08:47
— crashing the forecast for sixty seconds. In that unpredicted minute, with nothing
telling them what comes next, Jin-woo and Soo-ah finally get to **choose** each other.

---

### Why now
A funny, hopeful answer to algorithm-anxiety: the rom-com as quiet rebellion. Low
sci-fi budget (one city, practical light, no spaceships) with festival-friendly heart
and a universally relatable villain — the feeling that an app already knows you.

### Visual signature
Teal-and-amber: **cold predicted world vs. warm human error.** The warm tones win
more of the frame as they fall. Sticky-note wall as recurring motif. A smug AI
confirmation *chime* that grows audibly flustered.

### Logline
*In a near-future Seoul where an AI pre-plans everyone's day, a lonely analyst finds
the one moment it failed to predict and realizes a stranger broke the system on
purpose to meet him — so he sets out to become the most unpredictable man in the city.*
