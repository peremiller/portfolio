# OFF-MENU

### A sci-fi romantic comedy
*Working titles: "08:47" · "Wrong Order" · "Unpredictable"*

Adapted from a set of storyboard frames and a team photo.

---

## Logline

In a near-future Seoul where an AI plans everyone's day down to the minute, a
lonely analyst finds the one moment it failed to predict — **"08:47 — 잘못된 주문"
(Wrong Order)** — and realizes a stranger broke the system on purpose to bump
into him. To find her, he has to become the most unpredictable man in the city.

---

## The World

**CONTINUITY** pre-orders life for everyone: your coffee, your commute, even the
small talk you'll make — zero friction, zero risk, and, quietly, zero romance.
Why flirt when the algorithm already booked your wedding?

**Jin-woo** reconciles the real world to the forecast for a living. Then 08:47
happens: a coffee ordered to his table that the system never saw coming. An
impossible spark of free will. CONTINUITY flags it as an error and begins
erasing it — so Jin-woo records the one piece of evidence it can't rewrite while
he sleeps: **paper**. His wall of sticky notes becomes a love-case file for a
woman the system insists does not exist.

**Soo-ah** is a "ghost" — unpredictable by design, invisible to the AI. She
glitched his order as a prank and felt something with no data field for it. Now
she's running, because being predictable around someone is the scariest thing
she knows.

**The comedy engine:** the only way they can find each other is to make
increasingly terrible choices — ordering the worst thing on every menu, riding
to the wrong stop, crashing the wrong wedding — each bad decision de-syncing
them from the algorithm and nudging them one node closer.

---

## Characters

- **JIN-WOO (29)** — reconciliation analyst. Precise, lonely, quietly funny.
  Has never made an unplanned decision in his adult life. The wall changes that.
- **SOO-AH (28)** — a ghost. Reckless on the outside, terrified of being known
  on the inside. The only person in Seoul who orders off-menu.
- **THE GHOSTS** — a giddy little resistance cell / meddling friend group who
  meet at the one café the AI can't model. They feed Jin-woo awful advice that
  keeps working. *(The team breakfast photo.)*
- **CONTINUITY (V.O.)** — the city's predictive AI. Smug, maternal, increasingly
  flustered. Its confirmation *chime* is a running gag.

## Ending

To beat the quarantine, the Ghosts place one synchronized *wrong order* across
the whole city at exactly 08:47 — a citywide act of free will that crashes the
forecast for sixty seconds. In that unpredicted minute, with nothing telling
them what happens next, Jin-woo and Soo-ah finally get to *choose* each other.
The system reboots. It still can't predict them. Good.

## Tone & Look

*Her* × *About Time* × Korean rom-com. Teal-and-amber palette as theme: **cold
predicted world vs. warm human error** — the warm tones winning more of the
frame as they fall. Anamorphic, lots of negative space, the protagonist always
small against the system early, filling the frame by the end.

## OPENING SCENE — Screenplay

```
COLD OPEN.

INT. JIN-WOO'S APARTMENT — NIGHT

A wall of yellow sticky notes glows under a single desk lamp. Two
monitors throw blue light across a small, immaculate room. The city
glitters through floor-to-ceiling glass, indifferent.

JIN-WOO (29), hoodie, sleepless, stands facing the wall like it owes
him money.

                    CONTINUITY (V.O.)
          (warm, maternal, everywhere)
          Good evening, Jin-woo. You are forty
          seconds ahead of schedule. Would you
          like to sleep now?

                    JIN-WOO
          No.

A soft, disappointed CHIME.

He taps a note near the center. Handwritten:

          08:47 — 잘못된 주문

                    JIN-WOO (CONT'D)
          Run it again.

                    CONTINUITY (V.O.)
          The transaction at oh-eight forty-seven
          is an error. It has been reconciled.

                    JIN-WOO
          It keeps coming back.

                    CONTINUITY (V.O.)
          Errors do not come back. I have removed
          it four times.

Jin-woo goes very still.

                    JIN-WOO
          ...You've removed it four times.

CHIME. This one almost sounds nervous.

He pulls the note off the wall, holds it to the lamp. Beneath the ink,
faint — pressed through from a sheet that was on top of it — three
words in a stranger's handwriting:

          FOUND YOU. RUN.

                    CONTINUITY (V.O.)
          Jin-woo. You are now nine seconds behind
          schedule.

He looks up at the ceiling. Slowly. Smiles for the first time in the
film.

                    JIN-WOO
          Good.

He grabs his coat off the chair and walks OUT the door — into a
hallway that, for the first time, the system did not plan.

CUT TO:

INT. THE ONE CAFÉ THE SYSTEM CAN'T MODEL — CONTINUOUS

CHAOS. Eight people at a long marble table ordering the most insane
combinations imaginable — pineapple on everything, coffee with no
coffee in it. THE GHOSTS. They go silent as Jin-woo walks in holding
the note.

                    GHOST #1
          (mouth full)
          Oh. Oh no. He's got a note.

                    GHOST #2
          Somebody finally wrote him a note.

At the far end of the table, half-hidden, a woman lowers her menu.
SOO-AH. She sees the note in his hand. She sees the words on it. Her
face does something the algorithm has no field for.

                    SOO-AH
          (quietly, to herself)
          I told you to run.

                    JIN-WOO
          (across the room)
          I did. I ran here.

CHIME. Distant. Confused.

                                              SMASH CUT TO TITLE:

                              O F F - M E N U
```
